Tailtale — Project Proposal

Dogs can’t tell you what’s wrong.

In Thailand, dogs are treated like family — but they can’t self-report pain, hunger, or distress, and reliable vet access isn’t everywhere. Tailtale gives owners a same-day, camera-based read on their dog’s mood, and a clear next step when something looks off.
