02 — The Solution

Capture → Chart → Clinic

Tailtale is a browser app — no collar, no extra hardware. Point a webcam at your dog, and this loop runs end to end. It is the entire MVP.

STEP 01

Capture

Webcam or uploaded clip. The app samples 20 key frames — "align dog's face," start capture, done.

analyzer-camera-capture
STEP 02

Chart

A vision-language model reads the frames and returns an emotion category, a confidence score, and a trend line over time.

analyzer-results-dashboard
STEP 03 — CONDITIONAL

Clinic

Only if readings cross a concern threshold: nearby vet clinics appear on a live map, with distance, rating, call & directions.

vet-finder
