04 — Positioning & Validation

Why Tailtale Is Different — And Why We're Careful

The closest products monitor activity or vitals, or wait for the owner to type a symptom. None of them read a face. And we don't ship a category until we've measured it.

Competitive Landscape

Furbo / PetCube
Hardware camera
Remote video, treat-tossing, bark alerts. Detects that barking happened — not what the dog feels.
PetPace Collar
Wearable vitals monitor
Continuous temperature, pulse, respiration — clinically strong, but ~$780 over 3 years, and measures physiology, not expression.
PerkyPet / PetNexa
Symptom-chat apps
Owner types a symptom first. No camera-based, passive read of expression.
Tailtale
Browser app, camera-first
✓ Our approach
Live/recorded video → dog-specific facial emotion read → trend chart → clinic-finder when it matters.

How We Validate The Read

1

DogFACS-grounded prompting

The model scores specific action units before naming a category — not an open-ended "how does this dog feel."

2

Few-shot calibration

Labeled reference examples from two public datasets:

📁 Balico — Dog Emotion (Kaggle) 📁 Tanwar — Pet Facial Expression (Kaggle)
3

Held-out test set

~40–60 images never shown during prompt design, used only to measure agreement with the human-assigned label.

Pre-registered accuracy bar. If a category can't clear it, we fall back to fewer, coarser categories — e.g. "relaxed" vs. "worth a look" — rather than ship an unvalidated 5-way split.
