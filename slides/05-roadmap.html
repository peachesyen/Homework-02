05 — Roadmap

What Ships Now vs. What's Next

Everything below is real product thinking — the phases past the MVP are deliberately not built yet.

PHASE 1 — SHIPPING NOW

Course MVP

  • Upload / record a short clip
  • Emotion chart, conservative plain-language categories
  • Clinic-finder map, gated by concern threshold
  • No live-stream · no 3D avatars
PHASE 2 — PLANNED

Community

  • Stylized avatar generated from a photo
  • Community feed — post, like, comment
  • Lightweight moderation on shared content
PHASE 3 — LATER

Live & Playful

  • True live-video emotion analysis
  • Generated "bark reply" audio
  • Ships once the core pipeline is field-validated
12–24 MONTH HORIZON → Swap in dog-fine-tuned models as benchmarks mature · Opt-in vet-clinic booking partnership · Validation study with a veterinary behaviorist
