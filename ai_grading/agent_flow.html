Production Agent — Flow Diagram

How This Video Was Made

The actual pipeline that generated, critiqued, and stitched this deck — three stages fan out across all 6 slides in parallel; two run once, in order, because sequence matters there. All LLM calls use openai:gpt-5.6-luna via PydanticAI, with registered tools — not just structured output.

1 · Plan
agent: gpt-5.6-luna · tool: count_words
IN project_proposal.md
OUT SlidePlan (4–6× title, description, narration)
2a · Render
tool: Playwright + Chromium
IN slide HTML/CSS
OUT 1920×1080 PNG
2b · Synth Audio
tool: OpenAI tts-1-hd (Gemini TTS optional)
IN narration text
OUT WAV clip (≤16s)
↕ parallel · ×6 slides × 2 tasks
×6
3 · Critique
agent: gpt-5.6-luna (vision) · tools: contrast_ratio, count_words
IN PNG + narration text
OUT SlideCritique (visual + narration issues/fixes)
parallel across slides
4 · Enhance
visual: auto-patch · narration: gpt-5.6-luna reviser agent
IN critique + HTML/CSS + narration
OUT patched CSS and/or rewritten narration, or no-op
sequential · idempotent
×6
5 · Stitch
tool: ffmpeg (mux)
IN PNG + WAV pair
OUT per-slide MP4 segment
parallel across slides
6 · Assemble
tool: ffmpeg concat
IN 6 MP4 segments, in order
OUT full narrated video (~94s)
sequential · order matters
7 · Reel
tool: ffmpeg (atempo + setpts)
IN full video
OUT 30–60s highlight reel
